# Terrain, Routes, and Strategic Positions

> Terrain determines reachability, fire coverage, and attack timing. Check whether each unit can reach the objective before deciding which route is best.

## 1. Mountains

- On large, complicated mountainous maps, **Artillery Mechs, Minigun Mechs, and other Mechs that can cross cliffs** are often more mobile than conventional ground vehicles.
- Do not assume that Tanks and Mechs can use the same routes, or vice versa.
- Mountains can enable flanks, raids, and cliff-crossing attacks. Account for enemy electrical weapons and artillery counters.

## 2. Resource Deposits Inaccessible by Land

- Some deposits are on islands or separated from the mainland. Ordinary ground units and land Builders may be unable to reach them.
- Consider **Naval Builders**, transports, or other accessible expansion methods.
- Even if you do not claim a deposit, the enemy may establish a difficult-to-attack economic outpost there.
- When a move fails, verify terrain and the unit's movement type before repeating the same invalid order.

## 3. Islands and Coastlines

- Coastal deposits and forward islands are useful locations for outposts established by Naval Builders.
- Battleships can use their range to pressure lightly defended coastal positions, but cannot attack aircraft.
- Before establishing an overseas outpost, assess anti-air and anti-ship threats, construction time, and reinforcement routes.

## 4. Isolated Lava Islands

Three typical approaches:

1. **Secure the shoreline:** Trade artillery fire or use long-range weapons such as T3 Machine Gun Turrets. Land ground forces once the shore is controlled.
2. **Mass air assault:** Concentrate aircraft when enemy anti-air is weak and your air force is large enough.
3. **Forced airdrop:** Use transports or Flying Fortresses to insert ground units directly, accepting the risk of interception.

Choose based on enemy anti-air, shore defenses, and naval control. No approach is universally viable.

## 5. Attack Routes and Arrival Times

- **Fast units:** Interceptors, Heavy Interceptors, transports, and recon drones are often much faster than ground forces.
- During a long mixed-force attack, fast units may enter enemy fire first and be destroyed separately. Assemble at a safe rally point when simultaneous arrival matters.
- Before entering artillery range, consider bypassing the position, spreading out, or countering it from range instead of pushing forward in a dense column.
- Forward defensive lines need sustainable reinforcements and protection for construction. Do not send isolated Builders into enemy firing range.
